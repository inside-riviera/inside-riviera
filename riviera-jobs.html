A useful guide to working across borders

Check the work location

Italy, France and Monaco have different employment systems. Confirm which country the role is based in and where the work will actually take place.

Check language requirements

Some roles require Italian, French or English. Employers should state the languages needed and the level expected.

Understand the contract

Before accepting an offer, ask about the employer, contract type, working hours, pay, social contributions and any applicable cross-border arrangements.
