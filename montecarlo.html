Discover Monte Carlo

Where Belle Époque elegance, beautiful gardens and Mediterranean glamour meet.

Overlooking the Mediterranean, Monte Carlo is one of the Riviera’s most celebrated destinations. Famous for its iconic casino, luxury hotels and glamorous atmosphere, this Monaco district also rewards visitors with peaceful gardens, spectacular architecture and beautiful coastal views.

Whether you are arriving from Menton, Ventimiglia or Nice, Monte Carlo makes an unforgettable day trip or weekend escape.

1. Discover the Legendary Casino de Monte-Carlo

At the heart of Monte Carlo stands the Casino de Monte-Carlo, a masterpiece of Belle Époque architecture associated with Charles Garnier. Its grand exterior and opulent interiors make it one of Monaco’s most recognisable landmarks.

Place du Casino is framed by elegant buildings including the Hôtel de Paris and the Café de Paris. Even without entering the gaming rooms, the square is worth visiting for its architecture, gardens and lively atmosphere.

Inside Riviera tip: Visit Casino Square early for a quieter atmosphere. Check entry rules and dress requirements if you plan to go inside.

2. Wander Through Monte Carlo’s Beautiful Gardens

Monte Carlo has a quieter side among its landscaped green spaces. The Jardins de la Petite Afrique, close to the casino, offer palms, tropical planting and a pleasant place to escape the bustle.

Further towards the coast, the Japanese Garden combines ponds, bridges and carefully arranged stones in a peaceful setting. These gardens reveal a gentler side of Monaco’s famous urban landscape.

Inside Riviera tip: Pair a walk through the gardens near Casino Square with a visit to the Japanese Garden.

3. Follow the Famous Monaco Grand Prix Circuit

The Monaco Grand Prix is one of the world’s best-known motor races. Each year, the city streets become a challenging Formula 1 circuit, with memorable sections around Casino Square, the Fairmont Hairpin and the tunnel.

Outside race week, motorsport fans can walk along public sections of the route and imagine the cars navigating its tight corners. The race is an important part of Monaco’s identity and international appeal.

Inside Riviera tip: If visiting during Grand Prix week, check road closures and transport restrictions well ahead of time.

4. Enjoy Elegant Cafés, Shopping and Riviera Lifestyle

The streets surrounding Casino Square are known for elegant boutiques, beautiful hotels and café terraces. The One Monte-Carlo district brings contemporary design and luxury shopping to this historic neighbourhood.

You do not need an extravagant budget to appreciate Monte Carlo. Take a leisurely stroll, admire the façades, enjoy an espresso or pause to watch the atmosphere around the square.

Inside Riviera tip: Late morning and early evening are lovely times to enjoy the architecture and café terraces.

5. Discover Larvotto and the Mediterranean Coast

Monte Carlo’s coastal setting is one of its greatest attractions. A walk towards Larvotto reveals a lively beach promenade, restaurants and inviting Mediterranean views.

Nearby, the Grimaldi Forum hosts exhibitions and cultural events. Monaco’s steep terrain means the waterfront and upper streets sit at different levels, but public lifts and escalators help visitors move around.

Inside Riviera tip: Make time for a waterfront walk and use the public lifts to avoid unnecessary climbs.

Plan Your Visit to Monte Carlo

Location: Monte Carlo is a district of the Principality of Monaco, on the Mediterranean coast.

Best time to visit: Spring and early autumn for pleasant sightseeing; Grand Prix week is especially busy.

Getting there: Regional trains connect Monaco–Monte-Carlo station with Nice, Menton and Ventimiglia.

How long to stay: One day for Casino Square, gardens and Larvotto; a weekend for museums, dining and the wider Principality.
