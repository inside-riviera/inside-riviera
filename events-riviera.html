What's happening on the Riviera?

From Sanremo and the Riviera dei Fiori to Menton and Monte-Carlo, discover markets, concerts, festivals, family activities and seasonal celebrations on both sides of the French-Italian border.

Find events by town

The coast is made up of towns with very different calendars: weekly markets, village fêtes, food festivals, outdoor concerts, exhibitions and major annual celebrations. Start with the place you plan to visit, then check the organiser's information before travelling because dates, times and access can change.

What kinds of events can you find?

  • Markets: weekly town markets, antiques, vintage, crafts and seasonal stalls.
  • Food and wine: olive, citrus, local-produce and traditional food celebrations.
  • Music and culture: concerts, festivals, exhibitions, theatre and performances.
  • Family activities: public celebrations, workshops, parades and school-holiday events.
  • Seasonal highlights: Christmas lights and markets, carnival, summer evenings and autumn village fêtes.

Inside Riviera is an independent guide. Event details can change; always confirm important information with the organiser before setting out.
