How to read this estimate

Important: Le Calandre is a small, naturally changing beach. The amount of exposed sand depends on beach shape, recent storms, wave energy and sea level. A regional marine forecast cannot directly measure the width of the beach.
  • Favourable: the forecast suggests relatively calm sea conditions, which are generally more favourable for exposed beach.
  • Mixed: waves may periodically narrow the usable strip of beach.
  • Unfavourable: stronger forecast wave conditions may cover much of the shore.
  • The initial thresholds are indicative only and should be calibrated against real observations at Le Calandre before being treated as reliable beach predictions.

A note on safety and accuracy

This page is designed to help with beach planning, not to assess swimming safety. Forecasts can change and local conditions can differ from the model grid. Do not enter the water or approach the shore during rough conditions, and follow local warnings and restrictions.

Marine forecast data: Open-Meteo Marine Weather API. Forecasts are updated by their underlying weather-model providers at different intervals. This page requests the latest available forecast whenever it is opened and refreshes while it remains open.
