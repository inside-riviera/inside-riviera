Where to Play Golf on the Riviera

From hillside fairways above Sanremo to championship courses around Cannes and the Var, discover places to tee off during your Riviera stay.

Golf and the Riviera are a natural match. The mild climate makes much of the coast playable for a large part of the year, while the courses offer a remarkable variety of settings: olive groves, umbrella pines, Mediterranean hills and elevated views over the sea. Whether you want a full 18-hole round, a quick nine holes or simply somewhere to practise your swing, there are options on both sides of the French-Italian border.

This guide follows the coast from Menton and Monaco to Saint-Tropez on the French side, then from Ventimiglia to Imperia on the Italian side. A few extra Ligurian courses farther east are included as worthwhile day-trip options. Always check visitor access, tee times, green fees and course conditions directly with the club before travelling.

Where to play on the French Riviera

The Côte d'Azur has a particularly broad choice, from compact practice facilities close to the coast to established 18-hole clubs in the hills behind Nice, Cannes and Saint-Raphaël. The official Côte d'Azur tourism golf directory lists nearly thirty courses and practice venues across the region.

Menton · Short course and practice

Golf Club de Menton

Right near the Italian border, Golf Club de Menton is a handy place to practise without committing to a full round. It has a six-hole pitch-and-putt course, a practice area and putting greens, making it particularly suitable for beginners, short-game practice or a relaxed session while staying in Menton. It is not a full-length championship course, so plan a longer round elsewhere if that is what you want.

Find Golf Club de Menton · Côte d'Azur golf directory

La Turbie · 18 holes

Monte-Carlo Golf Club

Set high on Mont Agel above Monaco, Monte-Carlo Golf Club is one of the Riviera's most memorable golfing locations. The 18-hole, par-71 course sits at around 900 metres above sea level and rewards players with sweeping views towards the Principality and the Mediterranean. The mountain setting brings elevation changes and a more technical feel than a flat coastal course. Tee times are generally arranged in advance; check visitor conditions and the dress code before you go.

Monte-Carlo Golf Club website · Find the club

Nice · 9 holes and driving range

Golf Country Club de Nice

For a round without leaving the Nice area, Golf Country Club de Nice offers a compact nine-hole course and practice facilities on the western side of the city. It is a useful choice for holidaymakers who want a shorter game, a lesson or time on the range rather than a full day on a championship course.

Official Golf Country Club de Nice website · Find the club

Villeneuve-Loubet · Short course

Golf de la Vanade

Between Nice and Antibes, Golf de la Vanade is a practical option for beginners and players who want to sharpen their game. Its compact nine-hole layout is accompanied by a substantial driving range and teaching facilities. It is a good place to book a lesson or work on technique during a Riviera break.

Find Golf de la Vanade · Regional golf directory

Mougins · 18 holes

Golf Country Club de Cannes-Mougins

One of the best-known names in Riviera golf, Cannes-Mougins is an established 18-hole club founded in 1926. The course winds through a wooded landscape and is known for its carefully maintained fairways and technical challenge. It is a strong choice for experienced golfers looking for a classic club experience within easy reach of Cannes, Antibes and Grasse.

Official Cannes-Mougins website · Mougins tourism golf guide

Mougins · Golf resort

Royal Mougins Golf Resort

For a more exclusive golf day, Royal Mougins combines an 18-hole course with resort facilities, accommodation and a spa. Its setting in the hills gives it a peaceful atmosphere despite being close to Cannes. It is an appealing choice if you want to turn a round of golf into a longer stay or a special occasion.

Official Royal Mougins website · Mougins tourism golf guide

Mandelieu-la-Napoule · Historic course

Golf Old Course Cannes-Mandelieu

Close to the coast and the town of Mandelieu, the Old Course is one of the Riviera's historic golf addresses. Its setting among mature umbrella pines gives it a distinctive character, and its location is convenient for visitors staying around Cannes or Théoule-sur-Mer. Check which course configuration and tee times are available on your chosen date.

Official Old Course website · Find the course

Saint-Raphaël · 18 holes

Golf de Valescure

Further west, Valescure is a classic choice near Saint-Raphaël. Opened in the early twentieth century, it is known for its pine-fringed fairways and a more traditional parkland feel. It makes a good golf stop if your Riviera trip takes you towards Fréjus, the Esterel coastline or the Var.

Official Golf de Valescure website · Find the course

Roquebrune-sur-Argens · 18 holes and resort

Golf de Roquebrune

Between Cannes and Saint-Tropez, Golf de Roquebrune is an 18-hole course with broad views over the bay of Fréjus-Saint-Raphaël and the Massif des Maures. The resort setting, hotel and spa make it a good option for a golf-focused escape as well as a single round. The course is known for winding fairways and a technical layout.

Official Golf de Roquebrune website · Find the course

Sainte-Maxime · 18 holes

Golf de Sainte-Maxime

Overlooking the Gulf of Saint-Tropez, Golf de Sainte-Maxime is a scenic 18-hole option for golfers staying around Sainte-Maxime, Les Issambres or Saint-Tropez. Its hillside setting means the views are part of the experience, and the course offers a different perspective on the coastline from the beaches below.

Golf de Sainte-Maxime information · Find the course

Tourrettes · Two championship courses

Terre Blanche

In the Var hinterland, Terre Blanche is one of the region's flagship golf resorts. It has two 18-hole championship courses, an extensive training centre, hotel, spa and restaurants. This is a destination in its own right rather than a quick stop from the coast, but it is an excellent choice for a dedicated golf break or a special day out.

Official Terre Blanche golf information · Find Terre Blanche

Where to play on the Italian Riviera

The western Ligurian coast has a smaller golf scene than the Côte d'Azur, but it has two particularly appealing options close to Sanremo, plus a number of courses farther east. The Ligurian Golf Federation lists Sanremo and Castellaro among the clubs in the province of Imperia.

Sanremo · 18 holes

Circolo Golf degli Ulivi

Golf degli Ulivi is the best-known full-length course in the immediate Sanremo area. Founded in 1932, its 18-hole, par-69 course climbs across terraces on the hillside above the city, among olive trees and mimosa. It is a technical layout where accuracy matters, with views towards the sea and the Ligurian hinterland. The club also has an academy, pro shop and restaurant, making it a lovely place to spend a golf day.

Official Golf degli Ulivi website · Ligurian Golf Federation profile · Find the club

Castellaro · 9 holes

Castellaro Golf Club

A short drive inland from the coast between Sanremo and Imperia, Castellaro Golf Club offers a nine-hole, par-33 course in Mediterranean scrubland. The 2,300-metre layout is compact but not without challenge, with narrow fairways and undulating greens. It also has a driving range, putting green and short-game practice areas, plus a clubhouse terrace. A good option for a shorter round or for combining golf with a relaxed lunch in the hills.

Official Castellaro Golf website · Ligurian Golf Federation profile · Find the club

Staying around Ventimiglia or Bordighera? There is no full-size golf course in those towns themselves. Golf Club de Menton across the border is the closest compact practice option, while Golf degli Ulivi in Sanremo is a practical choice for a full 18-hole round. Castellaro is another option if you are happy to drive inland.

Worth the drive: more golf in Liguria

If you are travelling east from Imperia, Liguria has further clubs worth knowing about. These are outside the immediate Ventimiglia–Imperia stretch, but can suit a longer road trip or a golf-themed stay.

Garlenda · 18 holes

Golf Club Garlenda

In the Lerrone valley, inland from Alassio and Albenga, Garlenda is an 18-hole, par-72 course set among olive groves and pine trees. Designed by English architects John Morrison and John Harris, it opened in 1964. It is one of Liguria's established full-length courses and makes a worthwhile detour if you are exploring the western Riviera beyond Imperia.

Official Golf Club Garlenda website · Ligurian Golf Federation profile

Albisola · 9 holes

Golf Club Albisola

For a shorter game farther along the Riviera di Ponente, Golf Club Albisola is listed by the Ligurian Golf Federation as a nine-hole, par-32 course. It is a useful option to investigate if your route takes you towards Savona. Check current visitor access and course arrangements directly with the club.

Ligurian Golf Federation club directory · Find Golf Club Albisola

Arenzano · 9 holes

Golf Club Pineta di Arenzano

On the coast west of Genoa, Pineta di Arenzano is a nine-hole, par-36 course set in a green residential area above the sea. It is another possible stop for a longer Ligurian golf itinerary.

New to golf? Places to start

You do not need to book a full 18-hole round to enjoy golf on the Riviera. If you are a beginner, travelling with children or simply want to practise, consider these formats:

  • Pitch-and-putt: Golf Club de Menton is a compact six-hole option for short-game practice.
  • Nine-hole golf: Golf Country Club de Nice and Castellaro are useful shorter-course choices in their respective areas.
  • Driving range and lessons: Golf de la Vanade, Golf Country Club de Nice, Castellaro and the larger resorts have practice facilities; ask about beginner lessons and equipment hire.
  • First full round: Ask the club which course and tee options are suitable for your handicap and experience. Some clubs may require proof of handicap or a recognised golf licence.

Practical tips for booking golf on the Riviera

  • Book tee times in advance. Weekends, holidays and popular morning slots can fill quickly, especially in spring and autumn.
  • Check visitor rules. Some clubs are private or have restrictions for non-members, and access may depend on handicap or licence requirements.
  • Ask about equipment hire. Rental clubs, buggies and trolleys are not guaranteed at every venue. Reserve them when booking.
  • Bring suitable clothing. Many clubs enforce a dress code. Collared shirts and golf shoes are a safe choice; check each club's rules.
  • Plan for the terrain. Several Riviera courses are hilly, with elevation changes and narrow fairways. Ask whether a buggy is recommended or available.
  • Check maintenance days. Aeration, tournaments and seasonal maintenance can affect tee-time availability.
  • Confirm prices directly. Green fees vary by season, day, tee time and visitor status. Online prices can change, so check the club's own booking page.
  • Think about the drive. Many of the most scenic courses are inland or above the coast. Allow extra time on winding mountain roads.
Our quick picks: For a full round near the Italian border, choose Golf degli Ulivi in Sanremo. For a shorter Italian-side game, try Castellaro. For a compact practice session near Menton, Golf Club de Menton is convenient. For a classic French Riviera golf experience, look at Monte-Carlo Golf Club or Cannes-Mougins; for a resort stay, consider Royal Mougins, Roquebrune or Terre Blanche.

Make a golf day part of your Riviera holiday

One of the pleasures of golfing here is that a round can be followed by a swim, a stroll through an old town or a long lunch overlooking the Mediterranean. Play the hillside course above Sanremo and head down to the city for an evening by the sea; choose Menton for a compact practice session before exploring its gardens and old town; or make a day of a round near Cannes, Mougins or Saint-Raphaël.
