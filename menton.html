Discover Menton

The colourful jewel of the French Riviera — where French elegance meets Italian charm.

Nestled between the mountains and the sparkling Mediterranean Sea, just moments from the Italian border, Menton is one of the French Riviera's most enchanting destinations. Known as the Pearl of France, it is a place where pastel-coloured buildings cascade towards turquoise waters, lemon trees flourish under the Mediterranean sunshine, and life moves at a wonderfully relaxed pace.

Whether you're planning a weekend escape, a romantic getaway, or exploring the beautiful coastline between Sanremo and Monte Carlo, Menton deserves a special place on your itinerary.

1. Wander Through the Colourful Old Town

The heart of Menton lies in its historic Old Town, a charming maze of narrow cobbled streets, steep staircases, colourful façades and hidden squares. Warm shades of ochre, peach, pink and yellow contrast with blue skies and glimpses of the Mediterranean.

Make your way to the magnificent Basilica of Saint-Michel-Archange, one of the town's most recognisable landmarks. Continue uphill for panoramic views across the rooftops and coastline.

Inside Riviera tip: Visit early in the morning or during golden hour, when the light brings out the colours of the buildings.

2. Taste the Flavours of Menton

Menton is famous for its lemons. Its mild microclimate supports a rich citrus-growing tradition, celebrated during the annual Fête du Citron (Lemon Festival).

Situated at the meeting point of France and Italy, the town offers Mediterranean flavours: fresh seafood, homemade pasta, seasonal vegetables and olive oil. Look for barbajuans, savoury filled pastries, and pichade, a local speciality related to pissaladière.

Choose a waterfront restaurant and enjoy a leisurely lunch with a view.

3. Discover Menton's Extraordinary Gardens

Menton is often called the City of Gardens. Sheltered by mountains and blessed with a mild climate, it supports an extraordinary variety of Mediterranean and exotic plants.

Visit Jardin Serre de la Madone, a landscaped garden created by Lawrence Johnston, or Jardin botanique Val Rahmeh-Menton, known for its tropical and subtropical plant collections.

Inside Riviera tip: Allow time to explore slowly; the atmosphere is as special as the plants themselves.

4. Relax on Menton's Beautiful Beaches

With sparkling turquoise water and a picturesque coastline, Menton is ideal for enjoying the Mediterranean. One of its most popular spots is Plage des Sablettes, beneath the Old Town.

The beach's backdrop of colourful historic buildings and mountains makes it particularly photogenic. Enjoy swimming, sunbathing, a seaside lunch or a relaxed stroll along the waterfront promenade.

5. Experience the Riviera Lifestyle

What makes Menton special is not just its beauty, but its atmosphere. Compared with some of its glamorous neighbours, Menton offers a quieter, more intimate Mediterranean experience.

Life here is about simple pleasures: morning coffee on a sunny terrace, a stroll through the local market, an afternoon by the sea, or an aperitif overlooking the harbour.

Its location makes it a wonderful base for exploring Monaco and nearby Italian towns such as Ventimiglia, Bordighera and Sanremo.

Plan Your Visit to Menton

Location: Menton, Alpes-Maritimes, France, close to the Italian border.

Best time to visit: Spring and early autumn for pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds; summer for the beaches; February for the Lemon Festival.

Getting there: Regional trains connect Menton with Nice, Monaco, Ventimiglia and other coastal towns.

How long to stay: One day for highlights; two or three days for gardens, beaches and local cuisine at a relaxed pace.
