Where to eat the best pizza on the Italian Riviera

From Ventimiglia and Bordighera to Sanremo and Imperia: our guide to pizzerias worth seeking out, from classic wood-fired favourites to contemporary Neapolitan-style pizza — and one very local Ligurian speciality.

An independent Inside Riviera guide · Western Liguria, Italy

There is no shortage of places to eat pizza along the Riviera dei Fiori. The trick is knowing what kind of pizza you are in the mood for. You will find traditional Italian pizzerias, soft and airy Neapolitan-style bases, creative toppings and relaxed family-run restaurants. But the western Riviera has its own answer to the pizza question, too: sardenaira, the rich, tomato-topped Ligurian bake that locals will tell you is not quite pizza at all.

At a glance

  • Ventimiglia: Stella, Le Due Palme and Il Piccolo Bistro
  • Bordighera: Valdisogno
  • Sanremo: Senese, Motivo and Club 64
  • Imperia: La Rustica, Oasi and Peperetta Dispetusa
  • Near Imperia: A' Livella in San Lorenzo al Mare
  • For a local speciality: order sardenaira or pissalandrea

Ventimiglia: pizza by the sea and in the old town

At the Italian-French border, Ventimiglia has everything from busy traditional pizzerias to newer places with a more contemporary approach.

01
A dependable all-rounder

Stella Pizzeria Ristorante Bar

A well-known central Ventimiglia stop for pizza, pasta and a broader Italian menu. Its combination of dine-in, takeaway and a substantial restaurant offer makes it a practical choice whether you are staying in town or arriving by train. It is particularly useful when not everyone at the table wants pizza.

Good for: A relaxed meal with a choice of pizza and other Italian dishes. Find Stella on the map ↗
02
For sharing

Ristorante Pizzeria Le Due Palme

A long-established Ventimiglia name with a broad menu and a reputation for generous pizza formats, including pizza by the metre and Roman-style pizza alla pala. It is a good option for a group that wants to order several flavours for the table rather than one pizza each.

Good for: Families and groups; ask what sharing sizes are available. Find Le Due Palme on the map ↗
03
In the historic centre

Il Piccolo Bistro

Set in Piazza Cattedrale in Ventimiglia Alta, this is an appealing choice if you want to combine a wander through the old town with a sit-down meal. Its pizza has attracted positive feedback, including praise for the dough and more imaginative topping combinations.

Good for: A pizza stop while exploring the medieval lanes. Find Il Piccolo Bistro on the map ↗
04
Neapolitan style by the marina

Sudestada

Down by the newer marina, Sudestada offers a more contemporary waterfront setting and is noted for Neapolitan-style pizza. It makes a natural choice if you like a softer, puffed-up crust and want to make an evening of the harbour atmosphere.

Good for: Neapolitan-style pizza and a marina-side meal. Find Sudestada on the map ↗

Bordighera: a classic pizzeria address

Bordighera is better known for its palms, old town and seafront, but it also has a few pizza addresses worth knowing.

05
Traditional pizzeria

Ristorante Pizzeria Valdisogno

Near the entrance to Bordighera Alta, Valdisogno is a long-running restaurant-pizzeria with a notably extensive pizza menu. The choice is broad, so it suits diners who enjoy comparing toppings and finding something beyond the usual Margherita. Feedback is mixed as well as positive, so we would approach it as a big-menu, informal option rather than a destination for minimalist pizza purists.

Good for: Choice, a casual meal and a convenient location for the old town. Find Valdisogno on the map ↗

Sanremo: serious pizza and local character

Sanremo has one of the strongest pizza scenes on this stretch of coast, including specialist Neapolitan-style kitchens and traditional wood-fired restaurants.

06
For pizza enthusiasts

Senese

One of Sanremo's most talked-about addresses for contemporary Neapolitan pizza. Pizzaiolo Giovanni Senese brings a strong Campanian identity to the menu, while combinations draw on Ligurian ingredients and seasonal produce. It has also been highlighted for its Gambero Rosso recognition, making it a particularly interesting stop for people who take their pizza seriously.

Good for: Carefully made Neapolitan-style pizza and creative seasonal combinations. Find Senese on the map ↗
07
A taste of Naples

Spaccanapoli

If you are after a lively, distinctly Neapolitan atmosphere, Spaccanapoli is a name to put on your list. Its menu leans into the traditions of Naples, with classic pizza styles and fried specialities alongside the familiar favourites.

Good for: A casual evening and a more traditional Neapolitan mood. Find Spaccanapoli on the map ↗
08
Wood-fired classic

Pizzeria Club 64

Just a short walk from the Casino, Club 64 is a long-running central Sanremo choice known for wood-fired pizza and a broad restaurant menu. It is an easy option to fit into an evening in the centre, especially if your group wants the familiar pizzeria experience rather than a specialist tasting menu.

Good for: Wood-fired pizza in a central location. Find Club 64 on the map ↗
09
Pizza with a local twist

Motivo Sanremo

Part pizzeria and part osteria, Motivo pairs artisan pizza with Ligurian dishes in a distinctive music-themed setting. The menu includes both classic choices and more elaborate house combinations, and it is one of the places where you can try a sardenaira-inspired pizza as well as a conventional round pizza.

Good for: Creative toppings, a characterful setting and a mixed group. Find Motivo on the map ↗
10
Seafront setting

500Voltemeglio

A Sanremo pizzeria and fish restaurant with a seaside setting, known for long-proved dough and quality-focused ingredients. It is worth considering if you like a lighter-feeling base or want a few more creative options alongside classic pizza combinations.

Good for: A meal by the sea and a more contemporary pizza menu. Find 500Voltemeglio on the map ↗

Imperia and the coast towards San Lorenzo al Mare

Around Imperia, you can choose between simple neighbourhood pizzerias, more creative menus and Neapolitan-style pizza a little further west.

11
A local favourite

La Rustica

La Rustica regularly appears near the top of Imperia pizza recommendations and is praised for its straightforward, satisfying pizzas. It is the kind of place to keep in mind when you want to concentrate on the pizza itself without making the meal overly formal.

Good for: A relaxed, good-value pizza stop. Find La Rustica on the map ↗
12
A lively pizza night

Oasi La Pizza

In the Porto Maurizio area, Oasi La Pizza is a long-established name with a convivial atmosphere and a menu built around pizza and pasta. It is especially suited to groups and families who enjoy a sociable, informal dinner.

Good for: Groups, families and a lively meal. Find Oasi La Pizza on the map ↗
13
For different dietary preferences

Peperetta Dispetusa

This welcoming Imperia pizzeria-restaurant is known for its broad menu and for catering to different preferences, with vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free choices listed by restaurant guides. It is a useful option when a group needs more flexibility than a standard pizzeria menu can offer.

Good for: Mixed groups and diners looking for alternative dough or plant-based choices. Always confirm current allergen and cross-contamination arrangements directly. Find Peperetta Dispetusa on the map ↗
14
Neapolitan-style

A' Livella, San Lorenzo al Mare

A short way along the coast from Imperia, A' Livella specialises in Neapolitan-style pizza and is often recommended by diners looking for a soft, well-risen crust and bold toppings. It makes a good detour if you are exploring the coastal cycle path or the villages west of Imperia.

Good for: A pizza-focused evening and classic Neapolitan flavours. Find A' Livella on the map ↗
15
Creative pizza by the sea

Kilo

On the Borgo Prino seafront in Imperia, Kilo is known for a more inventive, gourmet approach to pizza. It is a good choice if you enjoy unusual combinations and a contemporary style rather than a strictly traditional Margherita-and-four-seasons menu. Gluten-free options are mentioned in customer feedback, but check availability and preparation arrangements before booking.

Good for: Creative toppings and a seafront dinner. Find Kilo on the map ↗

Don't leave without trying sardenaira

The western Riviera has its own great answer to pizza — and it is one of the most delicious things to eat in the region.

In Sanremo, it is called sardenaira. Around Imperia, you may hear pissalandrea; in Bordighera, pissadella; and in Ventimiglia, pissaladièra or a similar local name. Recipes vary from town to town, but the idea is a soft, airy, focaccia-like base topped with tomato, garlic, anchovies, Taggiasca olives, capers and oregano. Some versions include onion. Traditionally, it is not simply a pizza without mozzarella: it is a local speciality with its own history and identity.

For a taste of the local versions, look out for these bakeries and addresses highlighted in recent Italian food coverage:

Where to look for sardenaira and its local cousins

Sanremo: La Tavernetta, Enea Sardenaira and Storico Forno Barbruni.

Bordighera: Panetteria Mirella, known locally for its pissadella.

Taggia: Il Grano, for the local figassa or sardenaira tabiese.

Imperia: Madamadorè and Bakery Lanteri, associated with pissalandrea.

Ventimiglia: Panetteria Mondino, for the town's traditional pissaladièra-style bake.

Apricale: La Favorita, where sardenaira has been featured among traditional dishes.

Find La Tavernetta ↗ · Enea Sardenaira ↗ · Mirella in Bordighera ↗

Our advice: Buy a slice from a bakery and eat it warm. It is ideal as a quick lunch, an aperitivo snack or something to take to the beach. Ask for the local name in whichever town you are visiting.

A few tips for eating pizza in Liguria

Know which style you are ordering

Neapolitan: Usually soft and airy, with a raised rim and a tender centre.

Wood-fired classic: Often a more familiar round pizzeria pizza, cooked in a traditional oven.

Pizza alla pala or al metro: Longer formats designed for sharing; ask how many people a portion serves.

Sardenaira / pissalandrea: A regional bakery speciality, usually sold by the slice or by weight, and not the same thing as a standard topped pizza.

Go at Italian dinner time. Many pizzerias are busiest from around 19:30 onwards, particularly at weekends and during the summer. Check opening days carefully: some restaurants close one day a week or operate reduced seasonal hours.

Book for a summer weekend. Popular places can fill up quickly, especially those near the seafront or in the centre of Sanremo and Imperia.

Ask about the dough. If you prefer a thin, crisp base or a soft, puffy Neapolitan crust, ask what style the restaurant serves rather than assuming all Italian pizza is the same.

Check dietary needs directly. A menu listing gluten-free or vegan options does not automatically mean a separate preparation area. Contact the restaurant about ingredients, allergens and cross-contamination.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the best place for pizza on the Italian Riviera?

There is no single winner for every style. Senese in Sanremo is a strong choice for contemporary Neapolitan pizza; Club 64 is a central wood-fired option; and La Rustica is a popular straightforward choice in Imperia. The best pick depends on the kind of pizza you enjoy.

What is the local pizza speciality in Sanremo?

Try sardenaira, a traditional Ligurian bake topped with tomato, garlic, anchovies, Taggiasca olives, capers and oregano. It is usually found in bakeries and traditional food shops as well as some restaurants.

Is sardenaira the same as pizza?

Not exactly. It is a traditional local focaccia-like bake with a distinct recipe and history. Names and toppings vary along the western Ligurian coast, where you may see pissalandrea, pissadella or pissaladièra.

Do I need to reserve a table?

For a popular pizzeria on Friday or Saturday evening, or during July and August, booking ahead is sensible. Opening hours and reservation arrangements can change, so check directly with the restaurant before travelling.
