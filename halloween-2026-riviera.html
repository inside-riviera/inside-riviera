Halloween 2026 on the French & Italian Riviera 🎃

From costumes and family fun in Menton to music in Sanremo's old town and ghostly folklore in Triora, here's where to celebrate Halloween along the border.

Friday 30 October – Sunday 1 November 2026

Where to celebrate Halloween this year

Halloween falls on Saturday 31 October 2026, but the celebrations start earlier and continue into Sunday in some towns. We've gathered announced events from local authorities and tourism offices across the French and Italian Riviera, with practical details and links to the original organisers. Also included: Gorbio and Ospedaletti.

Whether you're visiting with children, planning an evening out or looking for a particularly atmospheric village, there's something to explore.

MentonFamilies

Menton fête Halloween

When: Friday 30 October · 1:30–4:00 pm
Where: Jardins Biovès, Menton
Cost: Free

A family-friendly afternoon in the heart of Menton, with games, entertainment stands, sweets and surprises. Dress-up is encouraged. The morning is reserved for leisure-centre children; the public programme begins at 1:30 pm.

MonacoFamilies

When: Saturday 31 October · 2:00–5:30 pm

Vallecrosia AltaLive shows & music

Halloween Vallecrosia 2026

When: Saturday 31 October · 6:00 pm–1:00 am (Sunday)
Where: Historic centre of Vallecrosia Alta, Vallecrosia al Mare
Cost: €10 advance ticket; €20 on the night, subject to availability. Free for children up to age 12, according to the organiser's listing.

Vallecrosia Alta's atmospheric old town becomes an open-air Halloween stage, with more than 50 artists, live performances, music and DJ sets, plus food and drink areas. A free shuttle service is advertised for visitors. The celebration is organised by CIV “Vallecrosia al Centro” in collaboration with the Comune di Vallecrosia al Mare.

Tickets and information: +39 347 2415508. Check ticket availability and shuttle details before travelling.

SanremoAll ages / evening

Halloween nella Pigna

When: Friday 30 & Saturday 31 October · From 4:30 pm on 30 October; evening programme on 31 October

How to plan your Halloween weekend

  • For younger children: Menton's Jardins Biovès, San Romolo's Piccoli Brividi and Beausoleil are good places to start. Always check age guidance.
  • For an atmospheric evening: explore Vallecrosia Alta’s live performances, Sanremo’s La Pigna programme or Triora’s folklore-filled historic centre.
  • For Monaco: read eligibility conditions carefully. Some municipal activities are specifically reserved for local residents or pupils.
  • For Triora: plan your journey in advance. It is an inland village rather than a coastal stop, and the ghost tour is separately ticketed.
  • Before setting off: check the organiser's website for last-minute changes, booking conditions and exact schedules.
