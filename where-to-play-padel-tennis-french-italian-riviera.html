Where to Play Padel and Tennis on the Riviera

From the courts of Menton and Nice to the tennis heritage of Bordighera and the padel clubs of Sanremo: places to keep your game going by the sea.

A Riviera holiday does not have to mean giving up your racket. The coast has a lively mix of traditional tennis clubs, modern padel centres and multi-sport venues where visitors can book a court, arrange a lesson or find a game. This guide follows the coastline from the French Riviera into western Liguria, highlighting useful places on both sides of the border.

One quick language note: the sport is usually called padel (rather than paddle). It is played in doubles on an enclosed court and is easy to pick up, even if you have never tried it before. Tennis remains deeply rooted in this part of the Mediterranean, with historic clubs, clay courts and coaching options.

Where to play on the French Riviera

On the Côte d'Azur, padel has spread well beyond the big cities. You will find dedicated padel centres as well as established tennis clubs that have added padel courts. The following options make useful bases along the coast.

Menton · Tennis and padel

Tennis de la Madone, Menton

A convenient municipal option in Menton for visitors who want to stay close to town. The municipal sports facility at 5 Rue Paul Morillot is listed with tennis courts and padel facilities. It is worth contacting the venue or the town's sports service to confirm public booking arrangements, current court access and prices.

Menton town website · Find Tennis de la Madone

Beausoleil · Tennis and padel

Tennis Padel Soleil

Just above Monaco, Tennis Padel Soleil is one of the most versatile racket-sport addresses near the Italian border. The club advertises six floodlit tennis courts and six floodlit padel courts, with some covered courts, plus a clubhouse and fitness facilities. It is a strong choice if you want both sports in one place or need the flexibility of covered play.

Official website and booking information · Menton Riviera & Merveilles visitor listing

Nice · Tennis and padel

Nice Lawn Tennis Club

A historic club in the Parc Impérial area, Nice Lawn Tennis Club combines a substantial tennis programme with padel. It is a good address for players looking for a traditional club atmosphere, coaching and a more established tennis environment. Ask the club which courts and visitor options are available for non-members.

Nice Lawn Tennis Club · Find the club

Nice area · Padel

Padel Tennis Nice Valrose and Ultra Fabron

For a padel-first session in Nice, two names to look up are Padel Tennis Nice Valrose, in the university district, and Ultra Fabron, on the western side of the city. Both are listed among the area's padel venues. Check the current court timetable and booking platform before setting off, particularly during university holidays and the summer season.

Antibes · Tennis, padel and pickleball

Tennis Club d'Antibes

Close to the centre of Antibes and accessible from the train and bus station, this is a particularly practical all-round choice. The club advertises 15 tennis courts, three padel courts and four pickleball courts, as well as group lessons, a tennis school and holiday courses. It is a good option for families or groups with different levels and interests.

Official Tennis Club d'Antibes website · Find the club

Antibes · Tennis and padel

Tennis Padel Club La Roseraie

In the Antibes–Juan-les-Pins area, La Roseraie offers both tennis and padel, with court hire, group sessions, individual coaching and courses. Its own information highlights a relaxed club-house setting. It is worth checking seasonal hours: the summer timetable can differ from the rest of the year.

Official La Roseraie website · Find La Roseraie

Mougins · Padel

Padel Riviera Mougins

If you are staying around Cannes, Mougins or the western hills above Antibes, Padel Riviera Mougins is a dedicated padel venue with doubles and singles court options, a club-house and places to relax after a match. It is a useful alternative to searching only in central Cannes.

Official Padel Riviera Mougins website · Find the club

Cannes · Tennis

Cannes Garden Tennis Club / Tennis Montfleury

For a tennis session in Cannes, look at the municipal courts at Parc Montfleury or Cannes Garden Tennis Club in La Bocca. Montfleury is a central, leafy option, while the Garden complex has a broad programme and a mix of court surfaces, including covered courts. Confirm the right site when booking, as these are separate facilities.

Where to play on the Italian Riviera

Western Liguria has a long tennis tradition, particularly around Bordighera and Sanremo. Padel is now part of the local sports scene too, with dedicated centres in Ventimiglia, Camporosso, Dolceacqua and Perinaldo as well as clubs farther east.

Ventimiglia · Tennis

Tennis Club Ventimiglia

A local tennis option in the Peglia sports area, with a tennis-school tradition and courts for recreational play and training. If you are staying in Ventimiglia or near the French border, this is a natural first place to enquire. Contact the club ahead of time to check visitor access, court availability and whether lessons can be arranged in English or French.

Find Tennis Club Ventimiglia

Ventimiglia, Camporosso, Dolceacqua and Perinaldo

If you are staying around the western end of the Italian Riviera, you do not have to drive as far as Sanremo to find a padel court. There are several facilities in and around Ventimiglia, in the Nervia valley and in the hills above the coast. This is a particularly useful cluster for residents and visitors based around Ventimiglia, Bordighera and Vallecrosia.

Ventimiglia · 4 padel courts

Le Logge Hub

Le Logge Hub is a dedicated padel venue on Via Cornelio Tacito in Ventimiglia, close to the Liceo Aprosio area. The centre is listed with four padel courts and has hosted organised tournaments. It is one of the most straightforward choices if you want to play in Ventimiglia itself. Contact the club to check court availability, racket hire and how to book as a visitor.

Val Roya Padel

Another Ventimiglia option is Val Roya Padel, on Corso Cuneo. It is listed with two padel courts and a pickleball court, making it an interesting choice if your group wants to try more than one racket sport. Check directly with the venue for current booking arrangements and visitor access.

The club also appears in the Italian Tennis and Padel Federation's 2026 team competition records, which list its playing venue at Via Rossini. It is a convenient option between Ventimiglia and Dolceacqua.

Dolceacqua · Tennis and padel

Tennis Club Dolceacqua

In the beautiful Nervia valley, Tennis Club Dolceacqua is a local club with both tennis and padel courts. The club is listed with two padel courts and two tennis courts, and the official Visit Dolceacqua tourism site confirms that the local tennis club offers padel as well as tennis. It is a particularly appealing choice if you are staying in Dolceacqua or exploring the medieval village. Non-members should contact the club in advance to arrange a court or coaching.

Address: Via Roma, 18035 Dolceacqua, Italy · Phone: +39 0184 195 7443

Perinaldo · Padel and multi-sport centre

Campo Sportivo Innocenzo Cassini

Up in the hills above the coast, Perinaldo has a municipal sports centre named Campo Sportivo Innocenzo Cassini. The local Visit Perinaldo website describes padel courts at the centre, alongside five-a-side football, a gym and other community sports facilities. This is a lovely option to know about if you are staying in the hill villages, but contact the local sports association or municipality to confirm how court bookings work for visitors.

Visit Perinaldo – Campo Sportivo · Find the sports centre

Local tip: For a quick padel match near Ventimiglia, start with Le Logge Hub or Val Roya Padel. For the villages inland, check Tennis Club Dolceacqua or the sports centre in Perinaldo. Camporosso has more than one option, so compare Padel Camporosso and Vallecrosia Academy before choosing.
Bordighera · Tennis heritage

Bordighera Lawn Tennis Club 1878

Bordighera has a special place in Italian tennis history. Its Lawn Tennis Club dates to 1878 and is widely described as Italy's oldest tennis club. The historic club is a lovely name to know if you are interested in the area's sporting heritage; ask directly about guest play and current court access before planning a match.

Find Bordighera Lawn Tennis Club

Bordighera · Performance tennis

Piatti Tennis Center

For serious training, the Piatti Tennis Center is an internationally known high-performance centre in Bordighera. It focuses on player development and works with competitive players, so it is not the same proposition as booking a casual holiday court. Contact the centre directly to discuss programmes, coaching and eligibility.

Sanremo · Padel and tennis

Padel Academy Sanremo

One of the clearest padel choices in the area, Padel Academy Sanremo has six padel courts in the Solaro district, plus tennis facilities and coaching. The club describes different court orientations and surfaces, and offers organised matches for players who arrive without a full group. That makes it especially handy for visitors who want to join a game rather than simply rent a court.

Sanremo · Tennis

Tennis Sanremo

Near Corso Matuzia and the seafront, Tennis Sanremo is a traditional club with a strong tennis identity, red-clay courts and coaching activity. It is a good one to consider if you want a classic tennis session in town rather than travelling inland to a specialist padel centre.

Imperia · Padel and tennis

Don Quique Padel Imperia

At the Circolo Tennis Imperia complex on Via San Lazzaro, Don Quique combines four outdoor padel courts with access to the wider club setting, which includes tennis courts, changing facilities and a bar-restaurant. There is also an indoor padel location at Pontedassio, inland from Imperia, which is worth considering when the weather is unsettled.

Worth a look further east: Diano Marina and the surrounding Riviera dei Fiori also have tennis and padel options. If you are travelling beyond Imperia, search locally for Diano Padel Academy and confirm the current venue details before booking.

Padel or tennis: which should you choose?

Choose padel if you want a social game that is easy to organise with friends. Most matches are doubles, the enclosed court keeps the ball in play, and many clubs offer organised sessions that can help solo travellers find partners. It is also a fun option for mixed-ability groups.

Choose tennis if you prefer a larger court, longer rallies and a more individual style of play. The Riviera is particularly rewarding for tennis fans who enjoy clay courts and traditional clubs. Some venues also offer private lessons, junior programmes and intensive training.

If you are new to both sports, ask about a beginner lesson or a social session rather than booking a competitive match. Clubs can often advise on equipment and the right level.

Practical booking tips for visitors

  • Reserve ahead in summer. Evenings and weekends can be busy, especially in July and August.
  • Check visitor access. Some clubs are member-led or prioritise lessons and teams. Ask whether non-members can book a single court.
  • Ask about equipment. Padel rackets are different from tennis rackets. Confirm whether rental rackets and balls are available before arriving.
  • Look for organised matches. If you are travelling alone or as a pair, ask whether the club runs open games, WhatsApp groups or level-based match-making.
  • Check the surface and shade. Outdoor courts can be hot in the middle of the day. Early morning and evening are often more comfortable in summer; floodlighting and covered courts vary by venue.
  • Confirm prices and opening hours directly. Rates, seasonal schedules and booking systems can change, and online listings are not always updated immediately.
  • Remember the border. Menton and Ventimiglia are close, but clubs operate in different countries and may use different booking apps, languages and payment methods.
Our quick picks: For padel near the border, start with Tennis Padel Soleil in Beausoleil or Padel Academy Sanremo on the Italian side. For a mix of tennis and padel, Tennis Club d'Antibes is a convenient choice. For a traditional tennis experience in Liguria, look at Bordighera and Tennis Sanremo.

Make a racket-sport day of it

One of the pleasures of playing on the Riviera is how easily a match can fit around the rest of the day. Book an early court, then head to the beach or old town for lunch. In Sanremo, combine a game with a stroll along the seafront; in Antibes, the old town and Port Vauban are close by; and around Menton and Beausoleil, you can finish with views over the Mediterranean.

Club facilities and visitor policies can change. The venues above were researched in November 2026 using club websites, local tourism information and sports directories.
