Discover Ventimiglia

Where medieval Ligurian streets, colourful markets and Mediterranean beauty meet.

Just across the French border, Ventimiglia welcomes visitors with an atmospheric medieval Old Town, lively markets, archaeological treasures and a stunning stretch of Ligurian coastline. Beyond its busy transport hub, the town rewards anyone willing to explore a little further.

Whether you're planning a weekend escape, a romantic getaway, or exploring the beautiful coastline between Sanremo and Monte Carlo, Ventimiglia deserves a special place on your itinerary.

1. Explore Ventimiglia Alta — The Historic Old Town

Perched above the Roya River, Ventimiglia Alta is the historic heart of the town. Its narrow lanes, stone archways and quiet piazzas reveal centuries of Ligurian life.

The Cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta and nearby Baptistery of San Giovanni Battista are essential stops. Wander slowly through the medieval streets and look for viewpoints towards the river and sea.

Inside Riviera tip: Explore in the late afternoon for softer light and a quieter atmosphere.

2. Discover the Famous Friday Market

Ventimiglia’s open-air Friday market is one of its best-known attractions, drawing visitors from both Italy and France for clothing, accessories and the bustle of a traditional border-town shopping day.

Food lovers should also visit the covered Mercato Annonario for fresh produce, local cheeses, olives, herbs and seasonal Ligurian ingredients.

Inside Riviera tip: Arrive early on Fridays and keep time for a coffee and a visit to the covered food market.

3. Visit the Hanbury Botanical Gardens

On the hillside at La Mortola, the Hanbury Botanical Gardens bring together remarkable plants from around the world and wide Mediterranean views.

Founded in the nineteenth century by Sir Thomas Hanbury, the gardens combine palms, succulents, historic terraces and winding paths. They make a memorable half-day outing outside the town centre.

Inside Riviera tip: Wear comfortable shoes: the hillside paths include slopes and steps. Check visiting hours before travelling.

4. Relax on Ventimiglia’s Coastline

Ventimiglia’s seafront offers beaches, swimming spots and a laid-back Mediterranean atmosphere. The town-centre coastline is convenient for combining a swim with lunch or a stroll.

Towards the French border, the Balzi Rossi area is known for its dramatic cliffs and prehistoric caves. The nearby archaeological museum adds a fascinating historical stop to a coastal excursion.

Inside Riviera tip: For Balzi Rossi, check access and beach conditions in advance; parts of the coast are rocky.

5. Taste Authentic Ligurian Flavours

Ventimiglia is a wonderful place to taste Ligurian cooking: freshly baked focaccia, farinata made from chickpea flour, and pasta with fragrant basil pesto.

Look for small trattorias and bakeries serving seasonal vegetables, olive oil and seafood. A leisurely lunch followed by espresso is a perfect way to experience the town.

Inside Riviera tip: Try a bakery in the morning for warm focaccia, then seek out a local trattoria for lunch.

Plan Your Visit to Ventimiglia

Location: Ventimiglia, Liguria, Italy, close to the French border.

Best time to visit: Spring and autumn for sightseeing; Friday for the famous market; summer for the coast.

Getting there: Regional trains connect Ventimiglia with Menton, Monaco, Nice, Bordighera and Sanremo.

How long to stay: One day for the Old Town and markets; two days to include Hanbury Gardens and Balzi Rossi.
