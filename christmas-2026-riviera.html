Christmas on the Riviera

The best Christmas trees, festive lights, markets and seasonal events on the Italian and French Riviera — from Sanremo to Monte-Carlo.

Home › Christmas on the Riviera 2026

Christmas on the Riviera has a magic of its own. Instead of snow-covered streets, the festive season arrives with illuminated town squares, palm trees, historic lanes, markets and the Mediterranean as a backdrop.

For 2026, we have picked out a festive route from Sanremo to Monte-Carlo, combining the most photogenic Christmas trees and light displays with local markets and events. Some programmes are still being updated, so check the organiser's page before setting off.

The Christmas tree trail: our favourite stops

Sanremo · from 5 December · tree lighting 6 December

1. Sanremo — the 12-metre tree in Piazza Borea d'Olmo

Sanremo is planning a major Christmas lighting display, with more than five kilometres of illuminated routes and over 4,000 points of light. The centrepiece is a 12-metre tree in Piazza Borea d'Olmo, with the main lighting ceremony expected on Sunday 6 December. Look out for the illuminated streets around Via Matteotti, Via Mameli and Via Palazzo, plus the large light installation in Piazza Colombo.

Also happening: Gust'Arte A Natale, 4–8 December, a festive event celebrating art, crafts and local products. The event is listed in the Riviera dei Fiori calendar.

Bordighera · 5–7 December and 12–24 December

2. Bordighera — a natural avenue of Christmas-tree shapes

For something different, walk along Lungomare Argentina beneath its avenue of more than fifty monumental Norfolk Island pines (araucarias). These tall, tiered evergreens are not decorated Christmas trees, but their distinctive silhouettes make a beautiful natural festive backdrop beside the sea.

From 12 to 24 December, Natale sul lungomare brings wooden chalets, artisan products, music and lights to the waterfront, daily from 16:00 to 22:00. A Christmas market is also listed for 5–7 December.

Ventimiglia · 1 December 2026–7 January 2027

3. Ventimiglia — an evening walk beneath the lights

Ventimiglia's Christmas lights are scheduled to illuminate the town centre and several hamlets from 1 December to 7 January, every day from 16:30 until 01:00. Look out for displays along Corso Genova, Via Cavour, Via Aprosio and Via Mameli, as well as around Piazza della Cattedrale, Piazza Sant'Agostino and Largo Torino.

Menton · 4 December 2026–3 January 2027

4. Menton — festive gardens, a Christmas village and nativity scenes

Menton's festive programme runs across the town, with the Christmas market at Mail du Bastion from 5 December to 3 January. The market brings together 27 traders and artisans, with food chalets and seasonal gifts. The inauguration is scheduled for 4 December at 18:00; the market is listed as open 10:00–19:00 Sunday to Thursday and 10:00–22:00 Friday and Saturday.

Make time for Jardins Biovès, the ice rink and the Chemin des crèches, a route of nativity scenes around the town. The official programme also lists festive parades, jazz concerts and family activities throughout December.

Monaco · from 29 November; Port Hercule village 4 December–3 January

5. Monte-Carlo — the spectacular tree in Casino Square

For the grand finale, head to Place du Casino. The 2026 Christmas programme announces a 20-metre tree, with the lighting ceremony scheduled for 29 November at 18:00. The square is the classic Monte-Carlo Christmas photograph, especially after dark when the decorations and casino façade create a dramatic setting.

For another impressive tree, the lobby of Hôtel de Paris Monte-Carlo is set to feature its traditional Chopard creation, more than eight metres tall. Hôtel Hermitage is also expected to present a special tree designed by Chanel Joaillerie. Hotel displays are subject to access arrangements, so check with each property before visiting.

At Port Hercule, Monaco's official Christmas Village opens on 4 December and runs until 3 January under the theme La Forêt enchantée (The Enchanted Forest), with food chalets, family activities and festive decorations.

Christmas on the Riviera 2026: frequently asked questions

Where can I see the best Christmas trees on the Riviera?

For landmark displays, head to Piazza Borea d'Olmo in Sanremo and Place du Casino in Monte-Carlo. For a seaside festive stroll, add Bordighera and Menton to your route.

When do Christmas markets open in Sanremo, Menton and Monaco?

Opening dates differ. The guide includes the currently listed 2026 dates for Sanremo, Bordighera, Menton and Monaco, plus smaller towns across the Riviera. Check the linked organiser pages before travelling, as programmes can change.

Can I visit the Italian and French Riviera Christmas markets in one trip?

Yes. Sanremo, Bordighera and Ventimiglia make a natural Italian Riviera route, with Menton and Monaco close by to the west. Allow time for traffic, parking and cross-border public transport.

Is there snow at Christmas on the Riviera?

Snow is unusual along the coast. The Riviera's festive atmosphere is more often made of palm-lined promenades, illuminated squares, mild seaside evenings and mountain villages in the hinterland.
