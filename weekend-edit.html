Our approach

The Weekend Edit is an independent discovery guide, not a ticket seller or event organiser. We select listings from the Inside Riviera events calendar and aim to make local discovery easier. Listings may change or be cancelled; always confirm dates, times, prices, access and availability with the organiser. If you spot an error, please contact us.
