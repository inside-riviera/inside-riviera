Who Are We?

We noticed that there isn't a single site collecting events from both the Italian and French Riviera. That's why we built Inside Riviera—created specifically for locals and tourists who love exploring both sides of the coast, experiencing different cultures, and seamlessly transitioning from a weekend in France to a week in Italy (or vice versa!).

We want to keep this platform dynamic and community-driven. We warmly invite locals, venues, and organizers to suggest their own events so everyone can discover hidden gems and vibrant happenings across the entire coastline.
